Delete Your Wisdom Walk Account

Account & data deletion request

You can permanently delete your Wisdom Walk account and its associated data at any time.

Request deletion by email

Send an email from the address associated with your account to initiate deletion:

Request Account Deletion

Or email us directly at:

info@wisdomwalk.live mission@wisdomwalk.live

Include the subject "Delete my Wisdom Walk account" and the email address you signed in with so we can verify your request.

What gets deleted

Data Type Deleted? Retention Timeline
Account profile (name, email, photo) Yes Removed within 30 days
Pastoral chat conversations synced to your account Yes Removed within 30 days
Device records linked to your account Yes Removed within 30 days
Saved verses & preferences stored on your device Yes Removed when you uninstall or clear app data
Anonymized/aggregated logs with no personal identifiers No May be retained for security & analytics

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Requests are completed within 30 days of verification.

Questions

If you have any questions, contact us at info@wisdomwalk.live or mission@wisdomwalk.live.
