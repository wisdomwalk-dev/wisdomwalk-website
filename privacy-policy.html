Wisdom Walk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wisdom Walk ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application. By using Wisdom Walk you agree to the practices described here.

1 Who we are

Wisdom Walk is a Bible reading and devotional app by wisdomwalk.live. For any privacy question or request, contact us at:

info@wisdomwalk.live mission@wisdomwalk.live

2 Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) to create and secure your account.
  • Device information. We collect basic device details (device model, operating system, and a device identifier) to keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and manage access.
  • App content you create. Saved verses, reading preferences, and settings. Most of this is stored locally on your device; account-related data may be synced to our servers.
  • Verse text for AI features. When you request an AI devotional or use the guidance chat, the relevant verse or message text is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a response.
  • Notification tokens. If you enable reminders, we use a push notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver daily verse notifications.
We do not collect your financial information, passwords (Google handles sign-in), precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data.

3 How we use your information

  • To provide and personalize the app (sign-in, saved verses, preferences).
  • To generate AI devotionals and pastoral guidance responses.
  • To send daily verse reminders you opt into.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

4 Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties who process data on our behalf, each under their own privacy policies:

Google Sign-In & Supabase Auth Used for user authentication and session security.
Google Gemini AI (Generative Language API) Powers devotional synthesis and pastoral chat generation.
Firebase Cloud Messaging Delivers daily verse and devotional push notifications.
Supabase Secure cloud database for account profile and device records.

5 Data retention & security

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. Data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). You may request deletion at any time.

6 Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your Wisdom Walk account and its associated data at any time:

  • On the web: visit our Account Deletion page.
  • By email: contact us at info@wisdomwalk.live or mission@wisdomwalk.live.

When you delete your account, we delete the personal data associated with it (account profile and device records). Requests are processed within 30 days.

7 Children's privacy

Wisdom Walk is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9 Contact us

Questions or requests regarding privacy and data protection:

info@wisdomwalk.live mission@wisdomwalk.live
